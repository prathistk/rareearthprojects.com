Privacy note

Draft for legal reviewWritten with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind

This note explains what happens to the details you share with Rarearth Projects Private Limited (“Rare Earth”, “we”) through this website, why we ask for them, and the choices you have.

Who is responsible

Rarearth Projects Private Limited, No. 2385, 2nd Floor, Next to Cauvery Public School, 60 Ft Road, E Block, Sahakarnagar, Bengaluru 560092, decides how the personal data you share through this site is used. For any question about it, write to info@rareearthprojects.com with the subject “Privacy”.

Grievance officer: To be confirmed

What we collect, and only when you choose to share it

  • When you send an enquiry: your name and phone number and, if you add them, your email address, the community and plot size you are interested in, a preferred date for a visit, your purpose and your message.
  • If you write to us as a landowner: where the land is, its survey number and extent, how you are connected to it, and anything else you choose to tell us.
  • If you write to us as a channel partner: your firm’s name, city and RERA agent registration number.
  • With every enquiry: the page you sent it from and, if you reached us through a link or an advertisement, the campaign tags in that link (such as utm_source) and the website that referred you. These show us which of our communications are useful.

We do not ask for identity documents, bank details or passwords anywhere on this website.

How your enquiry reaches us

This website does not keep your enquiry on a server of its own. When you press Send on WhatsApp, your message is put together in your browser and opened in WhatsApp; if you choose email, it opens in your email app instead. Nothing reaches us until you press send there. From that moment, WhatsApp (operated by Meta) or your email provider carries the message under its own terms and privacy policy, and it arrives at our team’s WhatsApp number or inbox.

If we later add a service that records enquiries directly from this site, we will update this note before we switch it on.

What stays in your browser

To make the site work smoothly, a few small items are kept in your browser’s session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab:

  • an unsent draft of your enquiry, so you don’t lose what you typed;
  • the first page of your visit and any campaign tags in the link you arrived by;
  • whether you have already seen the opening animation.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not place third-party trackers on this site. Our typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to load them, which shares your IP address with Google as part of that request. Google Maps content on a project page, such as a map or a 360° view, loads only if you choose to open it, and “Directions” and map links open Google Maps in a new tab.

Why we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a private viewing.
  • To send you the brochure, layout, prices or documents you asked for.
  • To follow up on your interest for as long as it is useful to you.
  • To tell you about new Rare Earth releases, only if you ticked “Keep me posted”. That box is never ticked for you.

We do not sell your details or pass them to anyone for their own marketing. We share them only where it helps us serve you, for example with a bank you ask us to introduce you to for a home loan or with our legal advisers when a purchase goes ahead, and with public authorities where the law requires it.

How long we keep them

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to respond and to follow up on your interest, and longer only where the law requires it, for example records of a completed purchase. Retention period: To be confirmed

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it once we no longer need it to serve you or to meet a legal obligation;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, including to updates, at any time; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in the event of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us and, if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, write to info@rareearthprojects.com or call +91 88841 50025. We will confirm who you are before acting on a request.

Children

This site is meant for adults considering land. We do not knowingly collect details from anyone under 18.

Changes to this note

If we change this note, we will publish the new version here with its date.

Terms of use

Draft for legal review

By using this website you agree to these terms. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the site.

Information, not an offer

This website describes our communities for general information. It is not an offer, an invitation to offer or a contract. A plot is reserved only through a written agreement signed by both parties, in line with the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and the rules that apply in Karnataka.

Accuracy

We work to keep this site accurate and current, but layouts, specifications, amenities, prices and availability can change, and some details here are indicative. Please confirm anything that matters to your decision with our team and with the documents shared with you at the time of booking. RERA-registered projects can be checked on rera.karnataka.gov.in.

Our content

The text, photographs, renders, plans, logos and project names on this site belong to Rarearth Projects Private Limited or are used with permission. You are welcome to view and share pages for your own reference; please do not copy, alter or use them commercially without our written permission.

Links to other sites

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, the Karnataka RERA portal and other websites are there for convenience. Those sites have their own terms and privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content.

Using the site responsibly

Please do not misuse the site, for example by sending enquiries in someone else’s name, attempting to break its security or collecting its content automatically.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss arising from reliance on this website alone, or from the site being unavailable at any time. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer protection or real estate law.

Disclaimer

Draft for legal review

Please read this before relying on anything you see on this site.

Photographs
Photographs show the site or its surroundings as they were when taken. Where a page shows photographs of another Rare Earth project, or stock photographs, they are captioned as such and do not show that project's site.
Drawings & renders
Layout plans captioned “Rendered from the layout drawing · indicative” are drawn from the layout drawings: plot lines and roads follow the drawings, while trees and colours are illustrative. Layouts captioned “Illustrative” are an artist’s impression only; they are not drawn from a layout and do not show the actual site. Images captioned “Architect’s render · proposed” show buildings that are planned and not yet built.
Plans & layouts
Plans are representations of approved layouts. Plot dimensions, areas and facing follow the sanctioned plan and may vary marginally on site. Plot counts and areas are taken from the layout drawings.
Prices
*Prices indicative & subject to availability; registration, stamp duty and other charges extra. Where no price is shown, it is available on request.
Distances & times
Distances and drive times are approximate and vary with route and traffic.
Maps
Schematic maps on this site are not to scale and show relative positions only.
Amenities & infrastructure
Amenities and facilities described as planned are subject to approvals and may change. Public infrastructure, such as road widening, ring roads or proposed economic zones, depends on government authorities and its timing is outside our control.
RERA
Where a community is registered under RERA, its registration number is shown with it and in the register below. Registration details can be verified on rera.karnataka.gov.in.
Not advice
Nothing on this site is financial, legal or investment advice. Phrases such as “appreciation potential” describe our view of a location, not a promise of returns.
