About Rare Earth

A developer that starts with the land.

Rarearth Projects Private Limited is a Bengaluru real estate company that acquires, approves and develops land. We create villa plots and plotted communities with clear titles, complete infrastructure and the kind of planning that holds its value.

Every project begins the same way: with careful due diligence on the land, its documents and where the region is heading. Only then do we design the layout, put in the roads, utilities and green spaces, and open it up to owners.

i.Acquire with care

Land is chosen for its location, its access and its long-term prospects.

ii.Approve fully

Conversion, layout approvals and RERA registration come before sales.

iii.Develop completely

Roads, drainage, power, water, parks and security are all delivered.
